# Image prompts

The visual direction for post images: abstract, a sunny forest day, cropped
unconventionally. Calm and low contrast, so the picture never competes with the
post text sitting under it on LinkedIn. The warm light doing the work is the
same hue as the product accent, `#E8590C`, which is why sunlight is in every
prompt and why nothing else in the frame is saturated.

Paste these into ChatGPT one at a time. The style block is identical in each so
the set reads as one hand.

## Style block

Reusable. Append it to any new subject to keep the set consistent.

> Abstract photograph. Sunny forest, mid-morning, light coming through the
> canopy. Unusual crop: the subject runs off the edge of the frame rather than
> sitting centred in it, and a third of the frame is empty. Soft natural light,
> low contrast, muted greens and greys with warm amber only where the sun lands.
> Shallow depth of field, 50mm. No people, no animals, no text, no logos, no
> paths leading to the horizon. Quiet enough to sit behind a paragraph of text.
> Square, 1:1.

## The eight

**1. Bark**
> Extreme close-up of tree bark, the trunk running diagonally out of both
> corners so no edge of it is visible. Sun striking one vertical ridge, the rest
> in shade. [style block]

**2. Canopy**
> Looking straight up into overlapping leaves, the frame cutting every branch
> off mid-span. One bright gap of sky in the lower third, the rest dense. [style
> block]

**3. The beam**
> A single shaft of sunlight through mist between trees. The beam enters top
> right and leaves the frame before reaching the ground, which is not in shot.
> [style block]

**4. Fern**
> One fern frond entering from the left edge, occupying the left third, sharply
> focused. The remaining two thirds are soft green shade with nothing in them.
> [style block]

**5. Puddle**
> A reflection of the canopy in still water, shot so the water fills the frame
> and the real trees are absent. Upside down, edges cropped hard, one amber
> highlight. [style block]

**6. Fallen trunk**
> Top-down view of moss on a fallen trunk, the trunk running horizontally out of
> both sides of the frame. Texture across the whole width, sun on the upper
> third only. [style block]

**7. Backlit leaf**
> One leaf backlit by the sun, filling three quarters of the frame and cut off
> at the top. Veins visible through it. The remaining corner is pale, out of
> focus sky. [style block]

**8. Undergrowth**
> The edge of a forest floor, most of the frame taken by out of focus
> undergrowth with a single sunlit stem in sharp focus off to one side. No path,
> no horizon. [style block]

## Where they go

LinkedIn renders a single image best at 1200x1200 for square. The board shows a
48px square thumbnail on the right of a card, per DESIGN.md section 3, not a
full bleed header, so the image has to survive being small: one subject, one
light source, no fine detail carrying the meaning.

When image generation is wired on Day 3, the style block becomes the fixed part
of the image prompt and the `visual` brand principle becomes the variable
part. That is what the `visual` principle is for.
